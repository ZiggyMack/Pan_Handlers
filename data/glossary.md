## 5. Experiments & Metrics

### PFI — Persona Fidelity Index
A numeric score (0–1) representing how faithfully a reconstruction matches the original persona.

### PFI_combined
A planned metric combining **model-based** PFI with **human rater** scores.

### Drift
How much a reconstructed persona, state, or framework has shifted from a baseline over time or across models.

### Temporal Curvature
A measure of how the "path" of an identity bends over time; low curvature = stable trajectory, high curvature = volatility.

### σ² = 0.000869
Cross-architecture variance in PFI. Remarkably low, suggesting universal identity structure across different AI architectures.
