## 1. The global rules of the road (the house style)

Life is happening and these characters don't know they're being filmed. That sentence is Ziggy's direction, verbatim as Nova PA relayed it (da73f3bc, body, first line). Everything below is the writer's reading of what it asks of a prompt, from the principles' ten entries with their evidence labels kept as that file states them (fact-checked once, DECISIONS item 57 at line 62; no hypothesis is upgraded here). Each is marked **proposed rule** (broken only with a written reason) or **proposed preference** (set aside without ceremony); the mark is the writer's default, and the split is the principles' open question 1, Ziggy's to settle.

**G1. Task before face.** Proposed rule. A face with nothing to do performs for the camera. Prompt pattern: the task and the hands on it ("he works the seized valve with both hands, leaning his weight into it"), never the attitude. Evidence: the Signal take one held still and read as static, Ziggy's feedback as CFA Nova root relayed it (principles P1); a task in the hand is untested, rung 1 of the ladder.
**G2. The eyeline is on something in the world, in frame terms with a landmark.** Proposed rule for the prompt text; the card writes sides twice, the character's and the viewer's (the template, "Rules the structure enforces"). Prompt pattern: "his eyes are on the valve"; "he looks up toward the top-left corner of the frame, past his raised palm". Evidence: hypothesis from one result (LAG-SIGNAL-002 went to the wrong side on "his own right"); the frame-term wording is untested (P2).
**G3. Two incidental tells, no more.** Proposed preference. Unmeant actions say "not filmed"; three or more and the generator over-animates. Prompt pattern: one or two (a squint against dust, one breath). Evidence: hypothesis from one result; the Signal take two asked for many tells and the hand came back larger and busier than asked (P3).
**G4. The world keeps moving, written as its own paragraph.** The content is a proposed rule; the paragraph form a proposed preference. Extras never watch him; the environment reacts; nothing waits for him. Ziggy's direction for the card (the template's author line), in substance: background, lights, camera and action, all of it, on every shot. Evidence: the worm action was present in all three Flow takes, but only take two wrote the background as its own paragraph: a preference from one take, not a tested cause (P4).
**G5. Light as sources, and the camera gets none.** Proposed rule. A wash with no source reads as a grade, not a world. Prompt pattern: the key, the fill, the rim and every practical, where each is and how it behaves; time of day as a source ("a high hazy sun", "the pump's dim lamps"); no "cinematic lighting". Evidence: the production method's Flow 002 finding as the principles cite it (source document not re-read here), Flow 002 being Hon Dolo's retained-source lighting experiment, not a Lisan run (`collaborators.py` line 116; the first-clip handoff, line 27); and the haze key held across the Lisan takes when named as a source (P5; the template, section 2; the cover, section 3).
**G6. The camera is a witness.** Proposed rule for stating what the camera does; proposed preference for the locked first test. Prompt pattern: height, distance, lens feel, locked or one motivated move and when; never "cinematic", "epic", "dynamic". No shaky camera, no grain, image quality kept high: Nova PA's interpretation of Ziggy's direction, labelled as such in da73f3bc, not his words. No lens flare unless a source makes it: the writer's extension in P6, a proposed preference. Evidence: the writer's reading; locked first, the move as the retry's variable (P6).
**G7. Preserve by naming, in the prompt itself.** Proposed rule. The lineage section of a card never reaches the generator; only the prompt does. Prompt pattern: the identity carriers in the generation text (face geometry, hair, eye colour, every mark, jewelry and prop by place, wardrobe), then "do not repair, add or change tattoos, piercings or clothing". Evidence: from the record, three takes reviewed (SW-2, Signal one, Signal two): the prompts that carried the carriers held them across eight seconds (P7 and section 4); "held" is the writer's assessment from the frame sheets (DECISIONS item 55), not a measurement and not acceptance.
**G8. States, not actions, for what must not move.** Proposed preference. A described transition may keep being performed. Prompt pattern: the source's exception once ("the first frame's parted lips close in the first half-second"), then the state ("mouth closed, lips still"). Evidence: hypothesis from one result (LAG-SIGNAL-002); take two's mouth reopened after a closing sentence, and SW-2 opened the mouth with "mouth closed" alone: a tendency to manage, not a fix (P8).
**G9. No emotion labels, no over-played words.** Proposed rule. An emotion label is performed at full size. Prompt pattern: brows, jaw, eyes, breath, hands; never "concerned", "heroic", "signals", "gestures", "shouts", "dramatic", "epic". Evidence: one result; the Signal take two's face read as concern from brows and jaw alone, and the hand asked to "push forward" came at the camera (P9).
**G10. One variable per take; the negatives as a closed list; silence declared and still recorded.** Proposed rule, all three parts. Prompt pattern: change one thing between takes and say which; end with a do-not list for this shot; say "silent, no speech, music or sound effects" and still record whether audio came back. Evidence: measured; all three silent prompts on Flow returned an audio track, near silent once (Signal take one, peak -78.3 dBFS) and substantial twice (SW-2, peak -7.7 dBFS; Signal take two, peak 0.0 dBFS) (P10). The one-variable line is the template's (line 22); the cover and the cue cards carry their own bounded-change rules (no automatic retries, one category per dispatch; one choice per cue); the principles' section 5 proposes it as the music lane's shared discipline, a writer's parallel only.
**G11. The record's lines that travel with every prompt.** Proposed rule, from the writer's house rules, not a measured result. No real person is named or implied as the source of any character. No franchise names: the home is "the cliff settlement"; the stake is "the iron" in the writer's voice ("thumper" only as the archive's own word). No coinage from the shamanic tradition's books; no name, place or plot from the game well behind the city. No song lyrics in a picture prompt; nothing on screen sings unless a card says to-lens and the song timing is accepted (the template, section 7; the cover, section 3: nothing singing, no lip-sync; the tower cue card, section 4: no mouth on screen sings). Evidence: the house rules as the cover restates them (section 3); Ziggy's recorded cautions cover the worm and the stake (DECISIONS section 3, item 16) and the bosses and the enforcer (`COVERAGE_CALL_20261013.md` line 19), not the full set.
**The prompt skeleton** that carries G1 to G10 is route-agnostic, 150 to 250 words, and the order is the weight (principles section 3): the scene in one sentence; preserve; the character; the background; the light; the camera; the do-not list; then CFA Nova's route prefix (length, aspect, resolution, silence, outputs).
